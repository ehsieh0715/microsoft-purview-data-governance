# Data Governance Roles and Responsibilities

## Data Owner

Accountable for the use, quality, security, and governance of data within a business domain.

## Data Steward

Responsible for maintaining business definitions, metadata, data quality standards, and resolving data issues.

## Data Custodian

Responsible for the technical storage, security, availability, and access controls of data platforms.

## Data Governance Analyst

Coordinates governance activities across domains, maintains governance standards, supports data owners and stewards, monitors data quality, and reports governance performance.

## Data Governance Forum

Provides cross-functional oversight of data governance priorities, data quality issues, policy compliance, and escalations.
